Payment and refunds

Card details are entered on Stripe’s checkout page and are never displayed to or stored by Stock Sentinel. Your account holds an email address and a credit balance — not a card number.

If something went wrong — a billing issue, a duplicate charge — contact us and we will make it right.

An eligible failed run returns its reserved credit automatically, so a scan that could not complete does not cost you anything. If a balance does not update as you expect, get in touch.

Why credits rather than a subscription

A scan draws on paid market and social data, so each run has a real cost behind it. Charging per run keeps that honest in both directions: you are not paying a monthly fee for a month you did not use it, and there is no incentive to pad the product with actions you do not need.

It also means you can try it once for $5 and stop, with nothing to cancel.
